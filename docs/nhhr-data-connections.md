# NHHR data connections reviewed 12 October 2026

The Zoer manifest is revision **93**, with 86 dataset resources, including one protected participant/consent collection omitted from the public catalogue. It identifies datasets and original files; scientific rows come from the actual selected originals through authorized access and SHA-256 checks. The Atlas combines **71 explorer views / 10,382 bounded source records**, with **42 short evidence stories and 62 chapters across nine guided routes**. These records have overlapping grains: directory entries, summaries and their supporting observations are not independent people, trials or journeys.

Explorer filters drive maps, charts, tables, exports and share links. Point area keeps the same-period source cohort scale when entities are hidden. Ambiguous observations at one site are withheld unless the dataset explicitly declares an additive metric: only the schedule event count opts into summing recorded events at a reference point. This exception does not aggregate hospital costs, percentiles, workforce rates or trial evidence rows. Nursing opens with Registered nurses; an explicit All-types choice persists in its URL.

Atlas maps use **vector Streets and Dark basemaps**, defaulting to Streets. The original campus map retains its existing imagery options. Provider failure leaves reviewed local geometry and source records available.

## Location and historical limits

Hospital points use official identity/address evidence and returned OpenStreetMap objects; PGMaps supplies read-only candidates. The hospital directory preserves all 61 original IDs while collapsing aliases to 57 rows. Its three unresolved descriptions receive no point. The 84-point union supports directory and cost views with different source coverage. Nats’oojeh Hospital's replacement opened **14 January 2025**: its current point represents the reporting unit, including source fiscal labels before and across that opening, rather than geolocating historical costs at the replacement building. The row's location note retains this limitation.

Trial locations use official BC Cancer, YMCA and HealthLink BC identities plus returned OpenStreetMap objects. The C042 raw sample has only three of the ten whitelist NCT IDs; all three reuse the same Prince George city-centroid coordinates, which are rejected. The PGMaps B.C. Cancer Agency in Vancouver is a different facility and is not substituted. No coordinates, communities or boundaries are inferred for the regional First Nations description. A historical study listing does not establish local enrollment, current recruitment, eligibility or site capacity.

Travel references preserve precision: town points are not boarding addresses, and even a facility point does not establish the exact boarding door or historical pickup position. Bundled UHNBC/KL and Terrace hospital/medical-centre labels retain town context. Mackenzie Junction and Small River (Rest Area) account for the six unresolved events. Source service-day strings, asterisks, stop-time text, sequence resets and separate arrival/departure records are retained. Connections split at unresolved events and follow recorded order; they provide no road geometry, distances, speeds, passenger counts or observed travel benefit. Local Prince George GTFS is a separate service.

## Remaining opportunities

- The 25-facility condition view includes 22 verified facilities outside Prince George. Additional unresolved source facility labels still need original NCT/site records, reviewed aliases and independent address evidence; city aggregates cannot be allocated to facilities or health areas using geometry alone.
- Province-wide profession/age profiles can support charts but cannot be assigned to Northern HSDAs or institutions from their provincial grain.
- Local GTFS context could accompany verified sites after a stated reference date and walking/network assumptions are reviewed. It must remain distinct from Northern Health Connections.
- Exact boarding addresses for coarse travel references, the three unresolved hospital directory labels, and the two unresolved schedule labels remain open. Facility-specific wait-time maps need original facility/procedure observations; the released Northern procedure table is an authority aggregate and its percentiles cannot be attached to individual hospitals.

## Trial condition mix and paired age maps

The [condition release](../public/data/nhhr/clinical-condition-mix.json) preserves 77 original city/domain rows across seven cities and thirteen exact NHHR coded condition labels. It is a ragged table: omitted combinations are not additional zero observations. All city study totals and historical RECRUITING totals reconcile to the existing comparator baseline. Shares retain the full source city denominator when categories are filtered. The separate recruiting composition share uses the full city RECRUITING denominator; it is not a within-condition recruiting rate. Repeated city totals are metadata and are not selectable measures. The aggregate CSV has no separate timestamp field: 29 April 2026 labels its alignment with the reviewed comparator baseline. [Provenance](../public/data/nhhr/clinical-condition-mix-provenance.json) and [review notes](../public/data/nhhr/clinical-condition-mix.README.md) retain original source IDs, hashes, classification and reconciliation.

The paired age view uses the existing 102 Northern LHA/year age rows. Each map has its own scale: `share_65_plus` in percent and `population_65_plus` in people. Camera, basemap and area selection are linked. Explorer links preserve `display=paired`, the source year and geographic filters; tables and CSV retain the same rows. Multiple periods remain available as records while ambiguous per-LHA values are withheld. The 2025 values remain projections.

No new service connection or boundary file is required for these views. Original three-digit LHA codes join the reviewed PGMaps LHA polygons; the 17 Northern codes and contextual geometry are checked. Trial city/domain rows join the existing seven reviewed city points through exact `query_city` labels. These city points are not facility locations or health-area polygons. Mapping trial condition counts to facilities outside the reviewed Prince George portfolio requires original NCT/site records plus verified facility addresses; allocating them to LHAs requires explicit compatible source geography. Boundary geometry alone supplies neither.

## Trial conditions at comparator facilities

The [facility-condition release](../public/data/nhhr/trial-facility-conditions.json) contains 171 observed facility/domain records and seven city coverage records derived from 15,028 unique original city/NCT records. The 25 reviewed points include 22 sites outside Prince George. The [study-site CSV](../public/data/nhhr/trial-facility-study-sites.csv) contains 4,401 deduplicated city/NCT/physical-site relationships; it is supporting evidence and must not be counted as an additional independent cohort. Of the original city/NCT records, 4,208 have at least one reviewed site and 10,820 have none; 200 are partly located with a nonempty unresolved recorded alias. These measures describe finite alias-review coverage, not the number of all facilities in a city.

Source totals and condition counts reconcile to the existing seven-city/77-row summaries. Site aliases are reviewed with operator addresses, named OpenStreetMap objects and PGMaps candidate records; Children’s/Women’s, UBC/VGH and former Tom Baker/Arthur Child identities remain distinct. Source-count/list disagreements are retained separately and do not manufacture additional facilities. The share denominator remains each facility’s full reviewed portfolio. A study may appear at multiple cities/sites, and historical overall statuses do not establish local recruitment or study-visit locations at every date.

No additional source access or boundary file was needed for this bounded release. Extending the unlocated records needs an unambiguous physical facility identity/address (and historical concordance where relevant), rather than a city centroid or an allocation from city totals. See [location and source provenance](../public/data/nhhr/trial-facility-conditions-provenance.json) and the [PGMaps use/mapping-gap review](../public/data/nhhr/pgmaps-data-audit.md).
