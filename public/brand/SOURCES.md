# NALS branding provenance

White and green NALS/UNBC SVGs are generated by `scripts/build-brand.mjs` from the pinned `packages/unbc-logo` submodule (https://github.com/ahzs645/unbc-logo). This uses the same renderer, department text, colour variants and embedded brand font as https://github.com/ahzs645/nals-wp/blob/main/showcase/logo.mjs.

Department: Northern Analytical Laboratory Services.
Artwork and trademarks belong to UNBC. These files are used for this independent design concept at the user’s request and do not imply university endorsement.

The logo embeds Helvetica Neue LT Pro Black from the kit. Page fonts, including the new BC Sans WOFF faces and helvetica-black.woff, are copied from the pinned NALS 1.0.1 repository by scripts/build-brand.mjs. The Helvetica Black faces are commercial fonts and retain their separate licensing terms; they are not relicensed under the application GPL. BC Sans is separately licensed under the SIL OFL (public/fonts/LICENSE_OFL.txt).

Theme adaptation source: packages/nals-wp at commit 53c8c991153c464a9449254c5aa788fd17b4d474 (version 1.0.1, matching nals-wp-main (1).zip), src/scss/_header.scss, _navigation.scss, _apply-button.scss, _search-toggle.scss, _typography.scss, _buttons.scss and _footer.scss. Theme author: Ahmad Jalil. Theme licence: GPL v2 or later.

The map deliberately uses a compact 112px desktop/64px phone masthead rather than the full WordPress header height. Gradient, two-column logo/navigation layout, typography, search tab, Apply link, and quick-link treatments follow the updated theme.
