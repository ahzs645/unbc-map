# NALS branding provenance

White and green NALS/UNBC SVGs are generated by `scripts/build-brand.mjs` from the pinned `packages/unbc-logo` submodule (https://github.com/ahzs645/unbc-logo). This uses the same renderer, department text, colour variants and embedded brand font as https://github.com/ahzs645/nals-wp/blob/main/showcase/logo.mjs.

Department: Northern Analytical Laboratory Services.
Artwork and trademarks belong to UNBC. These files are used for this independent design concept at the user’s request and do not imply university endorsement.

The logo embeds Helvetica Neue LT Pro Black from the kit. The page’s Helvetica Neue Black display font is supplied in nals-wp-main.zip. Both are commercial fonts and retain their separate licensing terms; they are not relicensed under the application GPL. BC Sans is separately licensed under the SIL OFL (public/fonts/LICENSE_OFL.txt).
