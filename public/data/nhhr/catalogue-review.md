# NHHR catalogue review · 12 October 2026

All 85 public NHHR collection inventories and dataset endpoints reviewed, with actual originals inspected by source family. Only selected measures are validated and released. Metadata, repeated source copies and model coefficients are not independent scientific observations. Protected participant/consent collections and personal records are excluded.

**85 collections · 71 explorer views · 10,382 loaded source rows.** Rows overlap across summaries and originals; this is not a count of distinct people, grants or studies.

## Mapping connections and remaining gaps

### Archived cancer incidence

Connected: official December 2015 Statistics Canada boundaries, matched by original DGUID/HR_UID pairs.

Three Northern HSDAs have archived polygons. The BC benchmark remains in charts and records without an overlapping province polygon.

### Combined North Coast and Nechako labour estimates

Connected: verified union of PGMaps 2021 economic regions 5960 and 5970.

Statistics Canada defines one combined LFS reporting area. Its source value is mapped once; blank source identifiers remain blank and the display key is explicitly derived.

### Finer community health maps

Connected: supplementary PHSA incidence and lifetime-prevalence rates for 29 publicly listed Northern CHSAs, joined by native codes to matching 2018 display boundaries.

The collected Zoer C016 extract has no CHSA scientific rows. New publisher measurements are attributed separately. Forty archived Ministry polygons from the Community Map of Canada mirror provide display context; current PGMaps polygons cannot replace the changed 2018 McBride/Valemount geography. Historical omitted cells and unreleased communities remain missing, and the mirror is used for visualization only.

### McMaster institutional comparator

Connected: verified main-campus reference at 1280 Main Street West, Hamilton.

The official university address agrees with independently retrieved OpenStreetMap campus geometry. Institution-wide totals are not allocated to a campus, individual project or nearby population.

## Publisher supplements

Publisher supplements are separate from the 85 collected Zoer catalogue entries. The 2011 NSERC recovery retains its collected C055 lineage and explicit mixed source origins.

### PHSA public source CDR2022

Two supplementary views contain 4,453 directly downloaded historical Northern CHSA registry rates and six explicit unreleased latest-period profile cells. All rates retain their native 2018 areas, Total sex category, original fiscal periods, 2011 Canadian Census age standardization and four separate disease definitions.

Forty archived Northern CHSA v2018 polygons provide context for 29 publicly listed measured CHSAs, using exact identifiers and names. Geometry is explicitly retrieved from the Community Map of Canada mirror attributed to BC Ministry of Health April 2019, rather than the unavailable historical official ZIP or the current PGMaps v2022 layer. McBride/Valemount remains one historical area. No health-authority values are allocated downward.

Remaining needs: A fresh direct official v2018 geometry archive would strengthen geometry provenance; its historical DataBC ZIP currently returns HTTP 404. Current CHSA observations would require independently reviewed periods, definitions and matching modern geometry. Confidence intervals and condition-specific non-release reasons are not provided by these downloads.

[Facility location review](facility-location-gap-review.json): six verified civic-address parcel references and eleven remaining source-specific location decisions.
